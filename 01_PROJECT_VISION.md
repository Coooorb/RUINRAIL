# RUINRAIL — Project Vision

> **Status:** Approved V1 design specification.
> **Game language:** English. Planning discussions may be in German, but all player-facing text, code naming, comments, and Claude implementation specs should be English.

## Project Title

**RUINRAIL**

This is the approved V1 project/game title for documentation and implementation naming. A separate legal/trademark clearance step is still required before commercial release; that legal step is not a game-design decision.

## Non-Negotiable Rules

- No PvP.
- No character classes or active character abilities.
- The build comes primarily from equipment, not from class selection.
- Solo, Duo, and Trio are supported.
- Full at-risk loot loss applies on failed extraction.
- XP, Level, spent Skill Points, Banked Coins, and safe Storage persist through death.

## Elevator Pitch

A top-down 2D pixel-art PvE extraction roguelite for solo, duo, or trio play. Survivors leave **The Shelter**, ride the underground **Ruinrail** into procedurally assembled Sectors made from hand-authored rooms, clear enemies and hazards, loot equipment, defeat a boss, then face the central decision: **secure everything and Return to Shelter, or Descend Deeper into a harder Sector for better rewards while risking everything currently carried.**

## Inspirations

The project takes structural inspiration from extraction games such as ARC Raiders and Ravengard, fast readable top-down action from games such as Soul Knight, and a dirty retro-futuristic/post-apocalyptic mood associated with worlds such as Fallout. These are references for high-level feel only; RUINRAIL needs its own original visual identity, names, world, items, enemies, and assets.

## Genre Statement

- PvE only.
- Top-down 2D pixel-art action.
- Extraction roguelite structure.
- 1–3 online players.
- Infinite dungeon depth.
- Full carried-gear loss on failed expeditions.
- Permanent XP/level/skill progression.
- Persistent safe base and storage.

## Minimal World Premise

A civilization-ending event known as **The Collapse** left most of the surface and underground infrastructure in ruins. **The Shelter** is a surviving safe enclave connected to an old underground transport network now called **The Ruinrail**. Survivors use the network to reach abandoned Sectors, recover valuable equipment, and push farther into increasingly dangerous territory.

V1 does **not** require a story campaign, faction reputation, branching dialogue, or a large lore system.

## Core Fantasy

Find valuable gear, become stronger during an expedition, and repeatedly decide whether greed is worth the risk. The most emotionally important moment is not merely defeating the boss; it is standing at the post-boss Transit Car while carrying items the player does not want to lose.
