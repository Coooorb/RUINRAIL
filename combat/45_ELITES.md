# Elite Mini-Bosses

> **Status:** Approved V1 design specification.
> **Game language:** English.

Elites are short hand-designed mini-boss fights. They use a consistent moveset from start to finish rather than multi-phase boss logic. Only one Elite is fought at a time in an Elite encounter.

Stats below are pre-Depth/Co-op-scaling baselines.

## Ruined Metro

### Tunnel Stalker
Fast mutated melee Elite. Lunge, three-hit Claw Combo, and telegraphed Tunnel Rush with recovery.

### Railguard
Slow heavy security robot. Burst Cannon, fanned Rail Sweep projectiles, and Ground Shock AoE for close-range denial.

## Rustworks

### Scrap Executioner
Heavy humanoid industrial melee Elite. Heavy Cleave, Overhead Slam AoE, and Execution Charge into heavy strike.

### Crusher Unit
Industrial machine. Crusher Charge with wall-collision recovery, Scrap Barrage, and rectangular telegraphed Hydraulic Slam.

## Overgrown Labs

### Mutated Brute
Very high-HP mutant. Double Slam, telegraphed Mutation Leap, and short Roar Rush aggression window.

### Prototype X-7
Mobile ranged combat prototype. Energy Burst, radial projectile pulse, and telegraphed Blink Shot reposition followed by broad salvo.

## Reward and Spawn Rules

Elite completion guarantees Coins plus at least one item/equipment reward and uses a better rarity table than ordinary combat. Legendary remains rare. Early Depths typically have 0–1 Elite encounter; later Depths may have 0–2, never simultaneous.
