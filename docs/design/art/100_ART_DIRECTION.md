## Visual Goal

Readable, chunky top-down pixel action with an original dirty retro-futuristic post-apocalyptic world. Inspiration may include the gameplay readability of Soul Knight and the atmosphere of post-apocalyptic worlds such as Fallout/ARC Raiders, but assets, silhouettes, environments, UI, items, and lore must be original.

Gameplay readability beats realism. Enemies, projectiles, loot, hazards, and telegraphs must remain easy to parse against dark/rusty environments.

## Biome Visual Identity
- Ruined Metro: concrete, tile, rail infrastructure, cables, emergency lighting.
- Rustworks: rust, machinery, pipes, furnaces, scrap, warning markings.
- Overgrown Labs: damaged clean-tech, glass, terminals, bio equipment, plant/organic overgrowth.
