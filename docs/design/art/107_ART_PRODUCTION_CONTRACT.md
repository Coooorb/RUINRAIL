# RUINRAIL Art Production Contract

> **Status:** Repository production contract adopted by TASK 150.
> **Authority:** This document is operational. It does **not** change any approved art rule. Where it restates a number, `art/101`–`art/104` and `art/106` remain the source of truth, and `Assets/Game/Scripts/Editor/Production/ArtProductionContract.cs` is the machine-checkable form.
> **Game language:** English.

## 1. Purpose

TASK 153–175 produce final art in separate batches, authored at different times. This contract exists so every batch is reviewed against the *same* rules: the same import settings, the same readability bar, the same originality boundary. It is the checklist a reviewer runs, not a new art direction.

### The one documented refinement

`art/100` names "the gameplay readability of Soul Knight and the atmosphere of post-apocalyptic worlds such as Fallout/ARC Raiders". `art/106 §1` replaces that loose phrasing with an ordered hierarchy and adds a fourth reference:

1. **Soul Knight — strongest** reference for sprite construction and geometry.
2. **Zero Sievert — important** reference for gritty pixel-world finish and environmental character.
3. **Fallout 4 — strongest** atmospheric reference.
4. **ARC Raiders — medium** strength, for restrained industrial sci-fi accents.

This is a refinement, not a conflict: `art/100` said "may include", `art/106` says in what order and at what strength. **`art/106 §1` is authoritative on the reference hierarchy.** `art/100` remains authoritative on everything else it states.

No other divergence was found, so no approved rule required a change and none was made.

## 3. Technical Import Contract

Every final sprite, tile, prop, icon and effect frame in the release path must import with:

| Setting | Required value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Texture Type | Sprite (2D and UI) | It is game art, not a texture map |
| Sprite Mode | Single or Multiple, as the sheet requires | Directional sheets slice to Multiple |
| Pixels Per Unit | **32** (`GridConstants.PixelsPerUnit`) | art/101: one 32 px tile = one world unit |
| Filter Mode | **Point (no filter)** | art/106 §3: preserve hard pixel edges |
| Compression | **Uncompressed** | Block compression destroys pixel art |
| Generate Mip Maps | **Off** | Mips blur a pixel-perfect ortho camera |
| Alpha Is Transparency | On | Correct edge alpha at sprite borders |
| Max Texture Size | ≥ the sheet's real size | Never downscale a sheet on import |

This matches the path `PlaceholderTileAuthoring` already uses, so final art imports exactly as the placeholders it replaces. `ArtProductionContract.ValidateImportSettings` checks all of it.

**Never** upscale low-resolution art and smooth it. Author at final pixel size. A 3× view for review is a camera/zoom concern, never a baked asset change (1920×1080 is the clean 3× reference scale).

## 4. Per-Category Production and Preview Specifications

These describe how to author and how to review each category. They add no new content: every role is already enumerated in `TestResults/completion_asset_manifest.md` (regenerated by `CompletionAssetManifest`).

### 4.1 Player (TASK 153)

- Canvas ~32×48 px; 8 facings (N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW).
- 6 clip keys — Idle, Walk, Dash, Downed, GetUp, Death — × 8 facings = 48 clip roles.
- The body carries **no** baked firearm. The weapon is a separate sprite on the WeaponPivot (art/103), so one body set serves all 33 weapons.
- Silhouette test: at 1× against a Ruined Metro floor tile, head/torso/hands must still read, and the survivor must read as capable but vulnerable (art/106 §4).
- **Preview:** place the S and SE facings next to a 32×32 tile at 1× and at 3×. If the outline dissolves into the floor at 1×, the value contrast is wrong.

### 4.2 Normal enemies, Elites and Bosses (TASK 154–156)

- 6 clip keys — Idle, Move, Telegraph, Attack, Recover, Death — × 8 facings = 48 clip roles per actor.
- **Telegraph is the highest-value frame in the set** (art/103). It must be distinguishable from Idle and Move at a glance, from across a room, in every biome.
- Each of the nine normal archetypes needs a silhouette that states its gameplay role before any surface detail (art/106 §4): Grunt, Shooter, Swarm, Charger, Brute, Bomber, Shield, Sniper, Summoner.
- Elites must escalate the world, not recolor a normal enemy. Bosses must be identifiable at room scale before the attack starts.
- **Preview:** lay Idle / Telegraph / Attack side by side at 1×. If Telegraph is not instantly the odd one out, it fails regardless of how good it looks.

### 4.3 Weapons (TASK 157)

- 33 distinct silhouettes, one per `WeaponDefinition`.
- A class shares a visual family but must never become a palette swap (art/106 §5).
- Authored for a 360° pivot: the sprite is drawn in one canonical orientation and rotated by `WeaponVisualDriver`. Check it at several angles, not only pointing right.
- Legendaries may carry stronger accents, emissive parts or hazard markings while staying in-setting.
- **Preview:** all weapons of one class in a row at 1×. If two are distinguishable only by hue, the silhouette work is unfinished.

### 4.4 Items, icons and pickups (TASK 158)

- 72 item-family icon roles: 33 weapons, 9 armor, 16 accessories, 10 consumables, 4 ammo categories.
- Icons are read inside a rarity frame; the frame must not overwhelm the item silhouette (art/106 §9).
- World pickups and their icons must be recognizably the same object.
- **Blocker:** `ItemDefinition` currently carries **no icon reference**. TASK 158 must add that narrow seam before icons can be integrated. Recorded in `TestResults/completion_asset_manifest.md` (regenerated by `CompletionAssetManifest`).

### 4.5 Environments (TASK 159–162, 164)

- Five logic tile categories per biome — floor, floor detail, wall, obstacle, hazard — on the exact 32×32 grid, plus the per-biome prop/door/rail/decal/foreground/environment-VFX packages.
- Walls split into blocking lower geometry and upper/foreground portions so characters can move behind them (art/102).
- Biome mood per `art/106 §7`: Ruined Metro cool dirty gray-green; Rustworks rust-orange on dark metal; Overgrown Labs aged off-white invaded by desaturated green.
- Floors must stay quiet. A busy floor that swallows a bullet, a loot glow or a telegraph fails §2 no matter how attractive it is.
- **Preview:** tile a 10×10 floor field, drop a projectile sprite, a loot pickup and a telegraph marker on it, and view at 1×. All three must remain obvious.

### 4.6 UI and font (TASK 163)

- Dark metal/charcoal surfaces, worn edges, restrained rust/amber/green accents; chunky pixel frames with high information clarity (art/106 §9).
- Every important state uses colour **and** shape/icon/text — never colour alone.
- Focus states must be obvious for both keyboard/mouse and controller.
- The pixel font must carry a recorded source and licence. `UiKit.Font()` currently returns Unity's builtin `LegacyRuntime.ttf`, which is a placeholder.
- **Preview:** the longest real string each view can produce, at 640×360, not lorem ipsum.

### 4.7 VFX (TASK 171–172)

- 13 roles: 8 `CombatFeedback` kinds (muzzle, impact, explosion, melee, stagger, heal, status, loot_glow) and 5 `AttackMotion` telegraph shapes (Stationary, Dash, Projectile, Zone, Slam).
- Short and layered, never simulation-heavy. Smoke and debris may never hide an active hazard or projectile for long (art/104, art/106 §10).
- Projectiles are deliberately **larger and more readable than realistic** — the combat model depends on visible trajectories (art/104).
- **Preview:** fire into a crowded room and watch for the enemy telegraph. If the effect hides it, the effect is wrong.

## 5. Asset Review Workflow

Every batch runs these five steps in order. A batch that fails any step is `REJECTED`, not conditionally accepted.

1. **Originality check (§6).** Failing this stops everything else; do not review a derived asset for quality.
2. **Import check.** `ArtProductionContract.ValidateImportSettings` must pass with zero problems.
3. **Integration check.** Roles resolve, no missing-sprite or missing-material warnings, placeholder art for the same role is removed from the release path (`art/106 §13`).
4. **In-game readability check.** Reviewed in the actual running game at 640×360, not in an image editor (`art/106 §13.8`).
5. **Human approval.** Recorded at the TASK 152 / 165 / 176 gate. Only then may a role become `APPROVED`.

Steps 2 and 3 are machine-checkable and automated. **Steps 4 and 5 are not.** No validator, and no model, may mark a role `APPROVED`.

## 6. Originality and Reference-Boundary Check

Applied per asset, before any quality review. The asset **fails** if any of the following is true:

1. It was traced, painted over, or derived from a screenshot or sprite sheet of a reference game.
2. It reproduces a recognizable character, robot, weapon, vehicle, building, logo, faction mark, sign, icon or UI layout from a reference game.
3. It reuses a reference game's exact palette or sprite sheet.
4. It was recolored or geometrically modified from copyrighted source art.
5. Its provenance cannot be stated.

Passing means all of the following are true:

1. The asset is original RUINRAIL work.
2. Its provenance is recorded: who or what produced it, from what, under what licence.
3. It follows `art/106`, using references only for high-level qualities — construction, finish, atmosphere, accent.
4. It carries no third-party mark, name or branding.

**Provenance is mandatory metadata, not a formality.** `ArtProductionContract` requires an originality attestation before a role may leave `CANDIDATE`, and `ValidateAttestations` fails any role that claims `APPROVED` or `INTEGRATED` without one. This is a bookkeeping guarantee: it proves an attestation was *recorded*, and cannot and does not verify that the claim is true. That judgment is human.

### What the references are, and are not, for

| Reference | Take | Never take |
|---|---|---|
| Soul Knight | compact construction, simplified anatomy, strong silhouettes, action readability at gameplay scale | characters, costumes, weapons, tiles, effects, UI, proprietary shapes |
| Zero Sievert | worn, survival-oriented, utilitarian environmental character; grounded extraction feeling | geometry, palettes, props, layouts, lore, silhouettes |
| Fallout 4 | decay, salvaged retro-futurist machinery, oxidized metal, abandoned infrastructure, dust and old signage | recognizable assets, brands, UI, factions, props, iconography |
| ARC Raiders | restrained modern industrial sci-fi accents, strong machine silhouettes, clear luminous tech cues | recognizable assets or designs; glossy generic sci-fi |

The result must be unmistakably RUINRAIL.

## 7. What This Contract Does Not Do

- It does not change any approved gameplay rule, balance value or content count.
- It does not add art roles. `TestResults/completion_asset_manifest.md` (regenerated by `CompletionAssetManifest`) is the only role list.
- It does not judge quality, feel or fun, and does not approve anything.
