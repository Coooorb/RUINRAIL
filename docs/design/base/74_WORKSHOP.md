# Workshop and Base Upgrades

> **Status:** Approved V1 design specification.
> **Game language:** English.

## Non-Negotiable Rules

- No scrap/copper/wire/material inventories.
- No second currency.
- No crafting recipes or weapon durability/repair system.

The Workshop is deliberately small. It uses **Banked Coins** only.

## V1 Required Upgrade Lines

### Storage Capacity
Exact capacity/cost steps are defined in `base/71_STORAGE.md`.

### Trader Level
Exact offer-count, quality curve, and upgrade costs are defined in `base/72_TRADER.md` and `base/77_ECONOMY.md`.

Additional Workshop upgrade lines may only be added by an explicit future design task. Do not invent crafting-material systems or extra currencies to make the Workshop feel larger.
