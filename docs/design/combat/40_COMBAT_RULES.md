# Combat Rules

> **Status:** Approved design specification unless explicitly marked as tunable.
> **Game language:** English. Planning discussions may be in German, but all player-facing text, code naming, comments, and Claude implementation specs should be English.
## Fundamentals

- 360-degree aim.
- Visible projectiles as the normal ranged model.
- No critical hits.
- No weak spots/headshots.
- Small random base damage ranges, e.g. 7–9.
- Friendly fire is fully disabled for player weapons, melee, explosions, grenades, and DoT.
- Environmental hazards can damage all players.
- No stamina.
- No durability.

## Readability

Powerful enemy attacks must be telegraphed. Combat difficulty should come from positioning, enemy combinations, projectile pressure, and risk management rather than hidden or instant damage.

## Heavy Attacks

High-damage attacks may deal roughly 25–40 initial-reference damage when clearly telegraphed. Avoid true full-HP one-shots in normal intended depth ranges.
