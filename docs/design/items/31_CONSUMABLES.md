# Consumables

> **Status:** Approved V1 design specification.
> **Game language:** English.

## General Rules

- One active Consumable slot.
- Additional consumable stacks occupy backpack slots.
- Identical Consumables stack.
- Each Consumable has one fixed rarity.
- Consumable rarity indicates only rarity/drop frequency; it does not add Affixes, random stats, or Legendary mechanics.
- Defibrillator has zero drop chance in Solo.

## Final V1 Catalog

| Consumable | Rarity | Max stack | Effect |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **Bandage** | Common | 5 | Heal **25 HP** after a **1.5s** use action. |
| **Frag Grenade** | Common | 4 | **35–45** explosion damage in a **2.5-tile radius**. |
| **Medkit** | Uncommon | 3 | Heal **60 HP** after a **3.0s** use action. |
| **Combat Stim** | Uncommon | 3 | **+20% Movement Speed for 8s**. Use time: **0.5s**. |
| **Smoke Grenade** | Uncommon | 3 | Creates a **4-tile radius** smoke zone for **6s**. Normal enemies cannot maintain line-of-sight targeting through the smoke; Elites and Bosses are not blinded. |
| **Shock Grenade** | Rare | 3 | **15–20 damage** plus very high Stagger in its AoE. |
| **Damage Stim** | Rare | 2 | **+20% Weapon Damage for 10s**. Use time: **0.5s**. |
| **Armor Injector** | Rare | 2 | **+20% General Damage Reduction for 10s**, using the temporary DR cap. Use time: **0.5s**. |

| **Defibrillator** | Legendary | 1 | Revive one fully Dead teammate at **30% Max HP**. Consumed on successful use. |

Grenades use their normal throw animation instead of a separate consumable channel time.
