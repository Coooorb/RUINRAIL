# Global Stat Caps

> **Status:** Approved V1 balance specification.
> **Game language:** English.

These caps are applied after combining permanent Skills, armor base stats, Accessory Intrinsics, Affixes, and temporary effects unless a specialized rule says otherwise.

| Stat | V1 global cap |
|---|---:|
| Movement Speed bonus | **+30%** |
| General Damage Reduction | **40%** |
| General Damage Reduction while Armor Injector is active | **50%** |
| Weapon Damage bonus | **+50%** |
| Reload Speed bonus | **+40%** |
| Weapon Switch Speed bonus | **+50%** |
| Dash Cooldown Reduction | **35%** |
| Dash Distance bonus | **+30%** |
| Melee Attack Speed bonus | **+30%** |
| Projectile Range bonus | **+40%** |
| Projectile Speed bonus | **+40%** |
| Healing Received bonus | **+50%** |
| Blaster Cooling Rate bonus | **+50%** |
| Blaster Heat-per-shot reduction | **30%** |
| Bow Charge Speed bonus | **+35%** |
| Knockback Resistance | **50%** |
| Stagger Resistance | **50%** |

## General DR vs. Specialized Reduction

General Damage Reduction bonuses add together and are capped as above. Specialized reductions such as Blast Suit's Explosion Damage reduction are a separate modifier and apply multiplicatively to the remaining matching damage after general DR.

Example: 40% General DR leaves 60% damage. A separate 30% Explosion reduction then leaves 70% of that remainder, for 42% final incoming explosion damage (58% total effective reduction).
