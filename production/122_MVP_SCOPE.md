# Full MVP Scope

> **Status:** Approved V1 design specification.
> **Game language:** English.

A full MVP representing the intended game includes:

- 3 biomes.
- 11 weapon classes.
- 33 authored V1 weapon definitions: 22 regular weapons + 11 Legendary-only weapons.
- 9 armor families.
- 16 accessory families.
- 10 Consumables.
- 9 normal enemy archetypes.
- 6 Elites.
- 6 Bosses.
- **63 hand-authored Room Prefabs total**, 21 per biome, plus procedural assembly.
- Infinite Depth and scaling.
- Loot rarity/Affixes/Legendary specials/passives.
- Extraction/Transit loop.
- The Shelter, Storage, Trader, Skill progression, Workshop upgrades, Starter Kit.
- Save/persistence/full-loot-loss handling.
- Solo, Duo, Trio.
- Join-code Sessions using Unity Multiplayer Services / Relay / Netcode for GameObjects.
- Downed, revive, Dead, spectator, Defibrillator, Medical Station.
- Co-op scaling and Transit voting.
- 11 full music tracks plus required stingers and gameplay SFX coverage.

“MVP” here means the first version that genuinely represents the planned game, not merely a technical prototype.
